A grid is just a graph: every cell is a node, and its up/down/left/right neighbors are its edges. Instead of reading an adjacency list, we compute neighbors with dr/dc. Everything else (queue, visited marking, loop until empty) is identical to the BFS you already know.


Remember this — BFS on a grid
  1. Put the starting cell into the queue
  2. Mark it immediately
  3. Take the front cell
  4. Check 4 directions
  5. Check boundaries
  6. Check original color
  7. Change the valid neighbor
  8. Push the neighbor
  9. Repeat until the queue is empty
