Problem

Given an m × n board containing X and O, capture all regions surrounded by X. An O is captured if it cannot reach any boundary O by moving horizontally or vertically through other O cells.

Click any cell on the board to choose the “current cell” (untick Edit cells).

Diagonals are excluded because the problem defines connection only by shared edges. Adding 4 diagonal pairs would let an O “leak” to the border through a corner, giving wrong answers.
