Algorithm summary
  • Greedy rule: while the stack is not empty, st.top() > num[i] and k > 0, pop. A smaller digit in an earlier (more significant) place makes a smaller number.
  • Strict >: equal digits are never popped. No larger digit before a smaller one (increasing digits): leftover k removes from the end.
  • Edge cases: k = 0 (nothing removed) · k = length (everything removed → "0") · increasing digits · repeated digits · leading zeros · remaining removals after the scan.
  • Complexity: time O(n), auxiliary space O(n). Each digit is pushed and popped at most once.
