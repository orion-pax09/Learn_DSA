Every node has a position number, called its index. It starts at 0.

Pick a pos and see what it means:

pos is NOT given to your function.
Your function only receives head (the first node). pos is just LeetCode's way of telling us how the test list was built. Your code has to find the cycle without it.

How could we detect this?

We can't "see" the whole picture like you just did. We can only follow arrows. So: send two walkers down the list at different speeds.
